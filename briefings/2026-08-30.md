## Russia / Ukraine

This follows a six-day streak of strikes in the Kyiv region that killed an additional 12 people. Russian forces are reportedly preparing further "massive strikes" specifically targeting Ukraine's energy infrastructure. In a significant blow to civilian assets, Russian strikes have also destroyed 12 million books just before the start of the school year. In response to these ravaging drone and missile attacks, Ukraine is racing to develop new interceptors. Security concerns have extended into NATO territory, as Poland has opened a terror probe into a fire at a factory that supplies drones to Ukraine. Geopolitical pressure is also mounting from the Global South, with Indian Prime Minister Modi telling Vladimir Putin that the war must end for humanity's sake. Internally, Ukraine faces potential instability as a former Defense Minister has revolted, raising questions about whether President Zelensky’s system is fracturing.
