**Key developments:**
- The FBI and Coast Guard boarded two oil tankers bound for Texas after reports indicated their networks were potentially breached or hit by cyberattacks (AP News, CBS News, ABC7 New York).
- Iranian officials stated that hackers launched a cyberattack on a massive oil tanker bound for Galveston (KHOU).

- The FBI seized domains for DDoS-for-hire services, continuing a crackdown on ‘booter’ and ‘stresser’ services in the District of Alaska (Department of Justice (.gov)).

- NASCIO reported that state CIOs are confronting expanding critical infrastructure cyber risks amid local capability and governance gaps (Industrial Cyber).

**Analyst comment:**
The convergence of reported cyber incidents targeting maritime logistics and critical state infrastructure suggests a broadening threat landscape, though current reporting does not establish a coordinated campaign linking the tanker incidents, the nursing system outage, or the data breach. It is likely that the tanker incidents represent opportunistic or state-aligned cyber operations given the specific attribution by Iranian officials, but confidence in this assessment is low because the link relies on a single party’s claim without corroborating technical evidence from US authorities. The thwarted assassination plot is a distinct, high-impact event involving Russian actors, but no link to the cyber incidents is reported; treating them as part of a unified strategy would be an unwarranted inference from shared timing and geographic proximity. The leading alternative explanation for the tanker incidents is that they are uncoordinated criminal or hacktivist actions, distinguished by the lack of a unified operational signature or attribution across the two vessels. Confidence in the judgment that these are separate incidents is moderate, as it rests on the absence of reported coordination in the source material.

**Gaps / indicators:**
- Technical attribution of the tanker cyberattacks (e.g., malware signatures, IP origins) to confirm or refute the Iranian claim.
- Whether the FBI’s seizure of DDoS domains is operationally linked to the tanker incidents or the nursing system outage through shared infrastructure or actor profiles.
