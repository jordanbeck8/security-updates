## Domestic US Security

**BLUF:** A ransomware incident has disrupted IT systems and services in Ellis County, Kansas, while US federal agencies boarded oil tankers suspected of being compromised by foreign hackers (KSN-TV; TechCrunch).

**Key developments:**
- A ransomware attack disrupted services for Ellis County, Kansas, including IT systems, prompting an ongoing investigation by local authorities (KSN-TV; DysruptionHub; Hays Post).
- The FBI and Coast Guard boarded oil tankers heading toward the US coast that were suspected of being hacked by foreign hackers (TechCrunch).
- Foreign hackers targeted water systems in Colorado (Axios).
- A cyberattack on McKesson resulted in the theft of 6.4 million unique email addresses (The HIPAA Journal).
- CISA shared a low-cost cybersecurity strategy for hospitals (American Hospital Association).
- CISA flagged an actively exploited Cisco ISE flaw with no available workaround (MeriTalk).
- A new Settra ransomware variant was deployed in attacks on retail and manufacturing sectors (Infosecurity Magazine).

**Analyst comment:**
Current reporting indicates a high volume of disparate cyber incidents affecting US critical infrastructure and commercial entities, but no single actor or coordinated campaign is reported across these events. The boarding of oil tankers by US authorities represents a distinct law enforcement and national security action, likely driven by immediate maritime safety and intelligence concerns rather than a direct operational link to the domestic ransomware campaigns in Kansas or Colorado. Confidence in this assessment is moderate, as the items do not provide evidence linking the maritime interdiction to the specific ransomware groups targeting healthcare, water, or county government systems. The primary alternative explanation is that these are opportunistic, uncoordinated attacks exploiting different vulnerabilities (e.g., Cisco ISE flaws, ransomware variants) rather than a synchronized adversary strategy; distinguishing between these scenarios requires attribution data not present in the current reporting.

**Gaps / indicators:**
- Identify the specific threat actors or ransomware groups responsible for the Ellis County, Colorado water system, and McKesson incidents to determine if they share infrastructure or tactics with the actors targeting the oil tankers.
- Monitor for CISA advisories linking the exploited Cisco ISE flaw to the ransomware variants (such as Settra) used in the retail and manufacturing attacks to assess if a single exploit chain is driving multiple sectors.
