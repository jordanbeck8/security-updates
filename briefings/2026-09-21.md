**Analyst comment:**
Ukrainian forces are likely prioritizing high-visibility strikes on Moscow’s energy infrastructure and political centers during the election period to maximize psychological impact and international attention, a tactic that appears to be succeeding in disrupting the electoral narrative (Moderate Confidence). The convergence of drone attacks on Moscow and the assault on a refinery suggests a coordinated operational effort to exploit the political vulnerability of the election window, although the items do not explicitly link these specific attacks to a single command directive (Low Confidence). An alternative explanation is that these are independent, opportunistic strikes by different Ukrainian units exploiting the same temporal window rather than a centrally coordinated campaign; distinguishing between these requires intelligence on Ukrainian command-and-control communications during the election period, which is not currently available. The meeting between Zelenskiy and Ratcliffe in Ireland may indicate ongoing coordination on intelligence or operational planning, but no link is reported between this diplomatic engagement and the specific timing of the Moscow strikes.

**Gaps / indicators:**
- Intelligence on whether the drone strikes on Moscow and the refinery assault were planned as a single, synchronized operation or executed by separate units acting independently.
- Details on the specific outcomes of the Zelenskiy-Ratcliffe meeting in Ireland, particularly any directives regarding future asymmetric operations against Russian energy or political infrastructure.
