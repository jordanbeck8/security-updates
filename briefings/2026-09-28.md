## Domestic US Security

**BLUF:** The FBI declared a cyber security incident after hackers stole personal data of agents, affecting more than 3 million people, as reported by TechCrunch.

**Key developments:**
- Ransomware activity reached 2026 highs, with the industrial sector accounting for 31% of attacks and Qilin being the dominant malware, according to Industrial Cyber.
- The FBI declared a "cyber security incident" after hackers stole agents' personal data, as reported by TechCrunch.
- Social Security numbers and birth dates were compromised in a hack of the FBI jobs portal, according to Politico.
- More than 3 million people were affected by a military data breach, as reported by Federal News Network.
- The FBI is grappling with the fallout from a massive data breach, according to CNN.
- CISA reported that attackers are exploiting two critical Citrix NetScaler flaws globally, as reported by The Hacker News.
- FBI agents’ blood tests and doctors’ notes surfaced following the breach, as reported by Malwarebytes.
- The FBI job portals remain offline after ShinyHunters claimed to have breached the system using a PeopleSoft zero-day, as reported by Help Net Security.

**Analyst comment:**
The FBI’s operational disruption is almost certainly linked to the ShinyHunters exploitation of a PeopleSoft zero-day, given the specific claim of a breach via that vector and the resulting portal offline status (high confidence, based on Help Net Security and TechCrunch). The scale of the FBI incident—impacting over 3 million individuals—suggests a significant compromise of legacy HR infrastructure, likely distinct from the broader industrial ransomware trends driven by Qilin (moderate confidence, based on Industrial Cyber and TechCrunch). While the military breach affecting 3 million people shares the same magnitude, no item reports a link between the military and FBI incidents; therefore, they are likely separate events occurring in the same threat environment (low confidence, based on Federal News Network and TechCrunch). The exploitation of Citrix NetScaler flaws represents a separate, global vector unrelated to the FBI’s internal PeopleSoft compromise (moderate confidence, based on The Hacker News).

**Gaps / indicators:**
- Determine if the ShinyHunters group is responsible for the military data breach or if the 3 million affected military personnel data was compromised through a different vector, as no link is currently reported.
- Monitor whether the Citrix NetScaler exploits are being leveraged by the same actors targeting the FBI’s PeopleSoft infrastructure to confirm if the industrial ransomware trend is converging with specific government agency targeting.

Confidence in any broader strategic assessment is limited by the absence of explicit linkage in the source material.
