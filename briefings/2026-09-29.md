**Gaps / indicators:**
- Monitor for any subsequent public release of the stolen FBI or Pentagon personnel data, which would contradict the group's current pledge and indicate a change in operational intent or internal group dynamics.
- Assess whether the Pennington County ransomware attack involves the same threat actor as ShinyHunters by comparing technical indicators of compromise (IOCs) or ransom notes, as current reporting does not establish a connection.
