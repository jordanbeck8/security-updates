No link is reported between the carrier movement and specific Iranian proxy activities, and we must not assume the adversary interprets this buildup as Washington would.

- Specifics regarding the destination, expected arrival date, and operational mandate of the third carrier group are unknown; confirmation of its operational status and specific tasking would clarify whether this is a routine rotation or a reactive escalation.
