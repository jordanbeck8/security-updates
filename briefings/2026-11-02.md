**BLUF:** Taiwan announced plans to criminalize the smuggling of Nvidia AI chips to China (Asia Times).

**Key developments:**
- Taiwan stated it must maintain control over its own maritime information to counter Chinese pressure (Reuters).
- Beijing is increasing its fleet of Type 071 amphibious warships, a move interpreted as signaling a focus on Taiwan (South China Morning Post).
- Taiwan plans to criminalize the smuggling of Nvidia AI chips to China (Asia Times).
- China’s coast guard activities are reported to be tightening in waters east of Taiwan and affecting the Philippines (Center for a New American Security | CNAS; Baird Maritime).
- Taiwan has altered draft rules to add thousands of personnel to its armed forces (South China Morning Post).
- A Taiwan minister emphasized maritime sovereignty (Taipei Times).
- Taiwan asserts that China is claiming fake jurisdiction over eastern waters (Baird Maritime).
- China’s coast guard is tightening its grip in waters east of Taiwan (Baird Maritime).

**Analyst comment:**
Taiwan’s decision to criminalize Nvidia AI chip smuggling likely reflects a high-confidence assessment that Beijing is actively seeking to circumvent export controls to accelerate military AI capabilities, driven by the specific reporting from Asia Times. Concurrently, the reported expansion of the PLA’s Type 071 amphibious fleet and coast guard activities in eastern waters suggest a moderate-confidence judgment that Beijing is enhancing both long-range power projection and immediate coercive pressure, though no item explicitly links these hardware developments to the specific timing of the chip sanctions. The addition of thousands of personnel to Taiwan’s armed forces appears to be a direct response to these perceived threats, yet the evidence is low confidence regarding whether this is a coordinated, unified strategic shift or a series of disjointed reactive measures. A leading alternative explanation is that these are independent administrative and operational adjustments rather than a synchronized campaign; this would be distinguished by the absence of synchronized PLA exercises or diplomatic statements coinciding with the chip legislation.

**Gaps / indicators:**
- Monitor for specific details on the scope of the Nvidia chip sanctions, including whether they apply to existing stockpiles or only new exports, to assess the immediate economic impact on Chinese AI development.
- Watch for coordinated PLA naval or coast guard movements in the eastern waters that coincide with the implementation of the new maritime information controls, which would strengthen the case for a unified coercive strategy.
